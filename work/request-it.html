App Branding

Request It

A logo rebrand for one of the first apps designed to let guests request songs directly from their phones, removing interruptions at the DJ booth.

Developed in collaboration with several of Toronto's high-profile DJs, the concept needed to feel intuitive, relevant, and rooted in the culture of live events.

The icon carries a dual meaning. At first glance it reads as a hand raised to make a request. From another angle, it mirrors the motion of a DJ scratching a record, playful and connected to the experience.
